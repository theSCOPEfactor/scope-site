Use Scope as an app

Install Scope on your phone or computer. It opens in its own window with no browser bars and gets its own icon.

  • Computer (Chrome / Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or menu → "Install Scope".
  • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share button → "Add to Home Screen".

Your levels and journal always load live, so the app needs the internet. Only the look of the app is kept offline.

Settings are saved in this browser / app only. Your phone and computer can have different looks.
