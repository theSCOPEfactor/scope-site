Terms & Privacy

Plain-language terms for using Scope. Last updated 25 Oct 2026.

1. What Scope is

Scope is a private educational and informational tool. It shows price levels, stage and relative-strength readings, option-chain readings, a trading journal and planning sheets that are calculated automatically from historical market data using fixed formulas.

Scope is not investment advice. It does not give buy or sell recommendations, tips, research reports or portfolio management, and the owner is not offering any of these services. Any words such as "buy", "sell", "target" or "break out" are only the names of calculated levels, not instructions to trade.

2. No guarantee, your own risk

Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Trading and investing in securities, especially options and futures, can lose you all of your capital and more. Past results do not indicate future results. You alone decide what to do and you alone carry the result. Please speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting on anything you see here.

3. Your account

  • You register with your name, email and mobile number and confirm your email with an OTP.
  • A free trial of 5 days is given once per person. After that you need a paid plan of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, at the prices shown on your Profile page.
  • Payment is made by UPI and approved by the owner after the payment reference (UTR) is checked. Access starts or extends once it is approved.
  • Payments are for access during the chosen period. Refunds are only at the owner's discretion.
  • One account is for one person. Only one login is active at a time; a new login logs out the older one. Do not share your login.
  • The owner may block an account that is misused, and may change prices for new payments at any time.

4. Use of the content

The content is for your personal use. Please do not copy, scrape, resell or share Scope's levels, tables or screens with others, or use them to run a public or paid tip service.

5. Your data (privacy)

  • What we keep: your name, email, mobile, plan and payment records (amount and UTR), login times, support messages, and the journal entries and settings you type in.
  • Why: to run your account, send OTPs, approve payments, answer your queries and show you your own journal. Nothing else.
  • Sharing: we do not sell your data or show it to other users. Your email address is passed to the email service only to deliver the OTP.
  • We do not collect PAN, Aadhaar, bank or card details, or photographs.
  • Your journal is kept separately for each member. The owner can technically access the site's database for support and backups.
  • Deleting your data: raise a request on the Support page and your account and journal will be deleted.

These terms may be updated from time to time; continuing to use Scope means you accept the latest version. For any question, use the Support page after logging in.
