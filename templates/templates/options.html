How to read this Owner only

Aa section fakat tamne (owner) dekhay che. Members ne nathi dekhato.

Summary ma shu che

  • Highest Call OI strike: je strike par call options ni open position sauthi vadhu che. Call sellers (writers) ni pakad ahi vadhu hoy, etle ghani vaar upar na bhaag ma resistance tarike jovay che.
  • Highest Put OI strike: put writers ni pakad sauthi vadhu. Niche na bhaag ma support tarike jovay che.
  • PCR (Put OI ÷ Call OI): 1 thi upar = put position vadhu, 0.7 thi niche = call position vadhu. Ek ank par bharoso na karo, e fakat mood batave che. Aa page ma PCR fakat dekhadela strikes nu che.
  • Max pain: je strike par option buyers ne sauthi vadhu nuksan (sellers ne ochhu payout). Expiry na divase price ghani vaar tya taraf kheenchay che, pan e niyam nathi.
  • ATM straddle: ATM call + put no bhav. Expiry sudhi market je move apekshit rakhe che teno anuman (± points). Straddle moto hoy to market moti hal-chal ni apeksha rakhe che.
  • ATM IV: ATM option no implied volatility. Event (result, budget, RBI) pehla vadhe, pachhi ghate (IV crush).

Aa badhu OI par aadharit sanket che. OI ma khabar nathi padti ke buyer ke seller koni vadhu che, etle "resistance/support" ne pakka level na maano. Expiry na divase badlav jaldi aave che.

OI by strike

  • Moti blue (Call) bar = tya call OI vadhu. Moti gold (Put) bar = tya put OI vadhu.
  • Spot ni upar nu sauthi motu call bar ghani vaar upar nu "diwal" tarike, ane spot ni niche nu sauthi motu put bar niche nu "diwal" tarike jovay che.
  • Jo spot ek diwal ni nazdik pahonche ane OI ghatva lage (next view: Change in OI) to e diwal kamjor thai rahi hoy tevu sanket.
  • Dotted line = max pain, dashed line = spot.

Change in OI

  • Kal na close karta aaj nu OI ketlu vadhyu/ghatyu. Aaj ni navi position kya banti hati e ahi dekhay.
  • Call OI vadhe + price ghate/sthir: navi call selling, upar nu level majboot thatu hoy tevu.
  • Put OI vadhe + price vadhe/sthir: navi put selling, niche nu level majboot thatu hoy tevu.
  • OI ghate: position band thai rahi che (unwinding ke covering). Price sathe jovo: "Buildup" view ma.
  • Pehla divas ma "previous close" no data na hoy to khali dekhay; bijaa divase thi chalu thase.

IV smile / skew

  • Darek strike no implied volatility (OTM side no). Normal rite aakar "smile" jevo ke tirchho hoy.
  • Niche na strikes (puts) no IV vadhu: traders niche ni suraksha (put) mehngi kharidi rahya che, ghana divas ma aa normal che.
  • Upar na strikes (calls) no IV vadhu: upar na move ni apeksha / call mehngu, kyarek short squeeze ke event pehla.
  • IV potana ja itihas sathe sarkhavo. Aa page ma history nathi, etle roj note karo to samajvu saral.

Full chain table

  • Vachche strike, dabi bajoo Calls, jamni bajoo Puts. ATM strike highlighted.
  • ITM (in the money) options ma intrinsic value hoy che; OTM ma fakat samay no bhav. IV hamesha OTM side no vadhu vishwasniy che.
  • Volume ochhu ane bid-ask paheloo hoy tya trade karvu mehnu pade (liquidity ochhi).

Buildup

  • Price ↑ + OI ↑ = Long buildup (e contract kharidnar vadhya). Price ↓ + OI ↑ = Short buildup (e contract vechnar vadhya).
  • Price ↑ + OI ↓ = Short covering. Price ↓ + OI ↓ = Long unwinding.
  • Dhyan: call ni "long buildup" market upar ni apeksha, pan put ni "long buildup" market niche ni apeksha (ke suraksha) batave che. Contract pramane vaanchjo.

Scope levels vs option strikes

  • Tamara VLOW / VHIGH / Magnet level ni nazdik jo sauthi vadhu Call OI ke Put OI no strike aave to e level ne vadhu pushti male (be alag rite ek j jagya batave).
  • Overlap na hoy to bhe ne alag j jovo, jabardasti ek karvu nahi.
